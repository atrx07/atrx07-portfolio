# Source provenance

## GodUI Magic Tab

This preview imports the portfolio's existing adapted `src/components/godui/magic-tab.tsx`, derived from GodUI, and supplies new CSS. Keyboard/manual activation, native buttons, roving focus, and reduced-motion support are retained.

Upstream: https://github.com/LucasBassetti/godui

## Other dependencies

React, Three.js (including RoundedBoxGeometry and RoomEnvironment), GSAP, Lucide, and build dependencies are installed from npm with their supplied licenses. Cabinet Grotesk is served through the same Fontshare stylesheet already used by the portfolio. Do not represent its license as GodUI's MIT license.
